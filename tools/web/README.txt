CHANGING HANDS - offline edition
================================

Open "index.html" in any web browser (Safari, Chrome, Edge, Firefox) on a Mac
or Windows PC. Nothing needs installing and no internet connection or licence
is required. Keep this whole folder together: index.html needs the "pages",
"media" and "data" folders beside it.

Contents (left side)
  Click the small triangles to open sections; click a title to show it.

Search (top right)
  Finds records containing ALL the words you type, ignoring accents and
  capitals. Add * to match the start of a word: "baptis*" finds baptism,
  baptised, baptismal... If nothing matches exactly, word beginnings are
  tried automatically. Click a result to jump to it; the words are highlighted.
  A search can be bookmarked: index.html#search=your+words

Reading pages
  Blue dotted words and * marks are pop-up notes (footnotes): hover over or
  tap them. Pictures and maps open full size when clicked. Embedded documents
  open as PDF files. Use your browser's Find (Ctrl+F / Cmd+F) to search within
  a page, and "Previous / Next" to move through the infobase in order.

This edition was converted from the Folio Views infobase "ChangingHands.nfo"
